# Branch & Vine Christian Academy

> Branch & Vine Christian Academy is committed to cultivating a Christ-centered community in which students are nurtured, supported, and equipped to grow in truth, courage, forgiveness, and wholehearted zeal. As we learn to abide in Christ, we strive to build one another up, pursue wisdom, and reflect His character in all aspects of life and learning.

## Our Core Values

* **Community Support:** We intentionally encourage, assist, and uplift one another, seeking the success of every student as they develop toward their full potential. (1st Thessalonians 5:11)
* **Courageous Perseverance:** We approach life’s challenges with steadfastness and faith, choosing to respond without complaint and without fear. (Joshua 1:9)
* **Excellence Unto the Lord:** We pursue all tasks with diligence, integrity, and joy, doing everything as unto the Lord and embracing abundant life with zeal. (Colossians 3:23, John 10:10)
* **Restorative Relationships:** We practice humility and grace, being quick to apologize, quick to forgive, and eager to restore unity. (Matthew 6:14)
* **Truth, Wisdom, and Understanding:** We value honesty and seek wisdom and understanding in every circumstance, committing ourselves to truth in both character and conduct. (Proverbs 2:6-10)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**What grades will the school serve?**
The school will open with grade levels based on community enrollment and will expand as the student body grows. Final grade offerings will be announced as enrollment commitments are received.

**Is the school accredited?**
Yes. Branch & Vine Christian Academy will be accredited through the Association of Christian Schools International (ACSI), a nationally recognized Christian accrediting body.

**Will tuition align with the Indiana School Choice Scholarship (“Voucher”) program?**
Yes. Our goal is to set tuition as close as possible to the Indiana School Choice Scholarship amount so that most families can attend with little or no out-of-pocket cost.

**Who qualifies for the Indiana School Choice Scholarship?**
Eligibility is based on Indiana residency and household income. Many families qualify, including middle-income households. Once the state releases updated income guidelines for the upcoming school year, we will provide a simple eligibility chart for families.

**How do I apply for the School Choice Scholarship?**
Families apply through the school after enrollment. Branch & Vine Christian Academy will guide parents through each step of the application process once the state opens the scholarship portal.

**What will tuition be for families who do not qualify for the scholarship?**
Tuition rates will be published annually. Even for families who do not qualify for the voucher, we aim to keep tuition competitive and offer additional financial assistance when possible.

**Is there a registration or enrollment fee?**
Yes. A modest enrollment fee helps secure your child’s place and supports initial administrative costs. Fee details will be provided during the enrollment process.

**What curriculum does the school use?**
We use academically rigorous, biblically grounded curriculum aligned with ACSI standards. Instruction emphasizes wisdom, character formation, and joyful learning.

**What are the class sizes?**
Class sizes will be intentionally small to support individualized attention, strong relationships, and a nurturing learning environment.

**How can I express interest or begin the enrollment process?**
Families can complete our interest form or attend an informational meeting. Enrollment instructions will be provided as soon as applications open.

**What if I want to help, regardless of whether I enroll children in Branch & Vine Christian Academy?**
Help is absolutely welcome! We will need teachers, volunteers, and financial partners. If the Lord is stirring your heart to help give children a strong Biblical worldview and you feel led to serve in any capacity, please include your contact information on the survey. We will gladly reach out to you.
